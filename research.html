Airframe wing structures

PUBLISHEDVOLUME 15 · ISSUE 5 · MAY 2026

Structural Analysis of Basic and Advanced Airframe Wing Structures

International Journal of Science and Research (IJSR) · Pages 1520–1527

✦
Readers' Choice AwardMay 2026 edition
ABSTRACT / OVERVIEW

This study presents a comparative overview of basic and advanced aircraft wing structures. It considers wing configurations, their aerodynamic and structural characteristics, key wing components, and design trade-offs related to stability, manoeuvrability, drag reduction and performance.

This is a concise overview for the portfolio. Refer to the journal article for the complete published abstract, methodology and findings.
